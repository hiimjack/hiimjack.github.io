Passionate about infrastructure and automation, I combine my skills as a system administrator and DevOps engineer to design scalable, secure, and reliable environments. For over five years, I have been working in the management and modernization of on-premises and cloud infrastructures, with an approach focused on efficiency, collaboration, and reducing operational complexity.

I gained experience at Nohup, where I design and automate containerized environments based on Docker and Kubernetes, manage infrastructure using Terraform, and develop CI/CD pipelines to optimize release processes. I handle migrations and replatforming on public clouds such as GCP, AWS, and Alibaba Cloud, contributing to the standardization of architectures through Infrastructure as Code.

In my work, I always strive to strike a balance between technical control and intelligent automation. I believe in simplicity, system stability, and knowledge sharing as fundamental levers for building resilient and sustainable infrastructures over time.
